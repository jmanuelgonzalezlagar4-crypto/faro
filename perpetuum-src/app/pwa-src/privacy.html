Perpetuum Workshop — Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Versión en español

Summary

Perpetuum Workshop (Taller Perpetuum) does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device, offline, with no accounts and no trackers. Sponsors are shown without collecting any data.

Data saved (only on your device)

So that you don't lose your work, the app saves locally, using the browser's or the system's storage: the design you are working on, the designs you save in “My designs”, the photos of models you upload, your efficiency records, the challenges you have completed and your settings (theme, language, graphics quality and vibration). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear the app's data.

Photos of your models

If you upload a photo of your model to turn it into parts or to trace it, the app shrinks it and saves it only on your device. Finding the wheel and the weights in the photo happens on your own phone: the photo is not sent to any server or analysed outside your device. It is deleted when you remove it from the design or clear the app's data.

Files and images you share

If you use “Share an image”, “Share the design” or “Export to a file”, the app prepares the file on your device and shows you the system share menu. You decide which app or person you send it to. Perpetuum Workshop does not receive any copy.

Sponsors and contest

When it opens with a connection, the app downloads the list of sponsors and the contest information from its own website. No data about you is sent in that download. Sponsors are shown as a box with their name and a link: the app does not use ad networks or track what you do. If you tap a sponsor's link, their website or your email opens, and what happens there is up to them.

“Models to test at home” may contain affiliate links to shops such as AliExpress, always labelled as advertising. Each product's picture is loaded from the shop's server, which, like any website, can see your connection's IP address. If you tap a link and buy something, the Workshop receives a commission; the shop handles your data under its own policy.

If you decide to enter the contest, you yourself email the organiser a file with your name or nickname and the design of your machine. The organiser will use that data and your email address only to run the contest, as explained in its rules.

Data that is NOT collected

  • No personal data or device identifiers are collected.
  • There are no third-party analytics, ad networks or trackers.
  • No network requests are made during use, except to download the app itself, its updates and the list of sponsors.
  • There are no purchases, sign-ups or accounts.
  • No information is collected from children.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for any special permission. Vibration, if your device has it and you don't turn it off in the settings, is used only as touch feedback when placing and removing parts.
